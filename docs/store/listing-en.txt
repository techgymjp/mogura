June 21, 1582, before dawn. "The enemy is at Honnō-ji!"
The temple is in flames, and figures keep popping out of the holes. Whack them!
Strike down Akechi Mitsuhide and the rumored "masterminds" of the Honnō-ji Incident to earn merit.
But if you hit your lord, Oda Nobunaga in his white robe, it is all over.

■ WHO TO WHACK
• Akechi Mitsuhide (+5) - swings his sword at you
• Hashiba Hideyoshi (+2) - hops about waving his gourd
• Tokugawa Ieyasu (+2) - dozes with folded arms, then jolts awake
• Shogun Ashikaga Yoshiaki (+2) - writes secret letters and looks around
• An imperial court noble (+2) - hides a "ho-ho-ho" behind his fan
• A Jesuit missionary (+2) - raises his cross high
• A Mt. Hiei warrior monk (+2) - twirls his naginata

■ WHO NOT TO WHACK
• Mori Ranmaru (-3) - waves both hands: "I'm on your side!"
• Nobunaga in his white robe (game over) - dances with his fan, and flies into a rage if you hit him

■ THE THRILL OF THE STRIKE
• Every hit flashes a sword slash and sparks, and sends the figure spinning into the sky
• Strike down Mitsuhide and the whole screen flashes and shakes
• Hit in a row for combo bonuses. Let one escape and your combo breaks

■ THEY WON'T PLAY FAIR
• Feints: they peek out halfway and duck back down
• Teasers that look like they're coming out... but never do
• Quick pops, and double dips from the same hole
• Silly ones that just won't go back in
• The fire grows and they move faster as time runs out

■ A WORLD OF OLD JAPAN
• Figures drawn from historical portraits, animated in 12 hand-posed frames
• The great hall of Honnō-ji burns with sparks and smoke
• Music played on shakuhachi and shamisen, speeding up as the fire spreads

■ ALSO
• 30 seconds per game, with ranks from "Master of the Realm" to "It Cannot Be Helped"
• Best score saved, and results you can share
• Hit Nobunaga by mistake? Watch an ad to turn back time to just before it (once per game)
• Plays offline (ads and sharing need a connection)
• English and Japanese
